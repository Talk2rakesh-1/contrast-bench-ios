# Research and distinct scope — 2026-10-27

Reviewed all seven account repositories via GitHub metadata and READMEs: recall-ready-ios, agent-guard-eval, private-mark-ios, policy-change-lens, outage-kit-ios, queue-compass, and the Talk2rakesh-1 profile. ContrastBench adds a designer/developer utility rather than another lookup, redaction, or inventory app.

[W3C Understanding Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) explains the readability problem, thresholds, and why decisions must not round ratios. [Relative luminance definition](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#dfn-relative-luminance) supplies the sRGB calculation. These are technical references, not copied prose, code, icons, or assets. WCAG is a web standard; applying a color-pair formula does not certify a native app.

The MVP deliberately excludes image sampling because anti-aliasing and transparency require additional interpretation. No licensing uncertainty from third-party imagery, fonts, or datasets is introduced.
